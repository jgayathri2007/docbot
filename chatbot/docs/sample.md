# Acme Support FAQ (sample document)

## Returns
Customers can return any unused product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.
Orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping.

## Support hours
Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm IST, via chat and email.
